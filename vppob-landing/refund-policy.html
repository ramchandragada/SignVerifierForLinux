Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations are handled for services provided by VPPOB, a service operated by theGSTco.

1. Nature of our services

Our services involve the allocation of a commercial address and the preparation of documentation. Because significant work and third-party costs are incurred once a service begins, refunds are considered on the basis set out below.

2. Cancellation before work begins

If you cancel before we have allocated an address or begun preparing your documentation, you may be eligible for a full or partial refund, less any non-refundable administrative or third-party charges already incurred.

3. After work has begun

Once an address has been allocated and documents have been prepared, fees for that work are generally non-refundable, as the service has been substantially delivered.

4. Government decisions

Our fees cover the provision of a compliant address and documentation and our support during the process. They do not depend on, and are not refundable solely because of, the decision or timeline of the GST authorities, which is outside our control.

5. How to request a refund

To request a refund or cancellation, contact us with your details and reason. We will review each request fairly and respond within a reasonable time.
