The basics

What is a Virtual Place of Business?

A Virtual Principal Place of Business (VPPOB / VPOB) is a genuine, rented commercial address that lets you register for GST in a state where you don't have a physical presence. It's a practical, compliant way for online sellers to expand into new markets, place stock in local fulfilment centres, and serve customers faster — without the cost and commitment of a full office lease.

VPOB — Principal Place of Business

A compliant commercial address in your target state, used as the principal place of business on your GST application.

APOB — Additional Place of Business

Add a marketplace fulfilment centre or warehouse as an additional place of business under your existing GSTIN.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Virtual Place of Business legal for GST registration?

Yes. GST law allows you to register at a commercial premises for which you hold valid documentation such as a rent agreement, NOC and utility bill. VPPOB provides a genuine, verifiable commercial address along with that documentation.

Which documents will I receive?

You receive a No Objection Certificate (NOC), a rent agreement in your business name, and a recent utility bill — the standard set of address-proof documents required for GST registration.

How many states can I register in?

Our virtual addresses are available across 12+ Indian states, so you can expand into multiple markets from a single provider.

How long does GST registration take?

Timelines depend on the state and the GST department's processing. Once your documents are ready, we help you file promptly and support you through any verification.

Who is this service for?

It's designed for e-commerce sellers and growing businesses that need a GSTIN in a new state to stock inventory in local fulfilment centres or serve customers there.
