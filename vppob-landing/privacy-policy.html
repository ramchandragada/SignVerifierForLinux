Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VPPOB (“we”, “us”, “our”), a service operated by theGSTco, collects, uses and safeguards the information you provide when you use our website or enquire about our Virtual Place of Business and GST registration support services.

1. Information we collect

When you submit our enquiry or callback form, or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name;
  • Your phone number and email address;
  • The state in which you wish to register and related business details you choose to share;
  • Any message or information you provide in your enquiry.

We may also collect basic, non-identifying technical data (such as browser type and pages visited) through standard web analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you request;
  • To contact you by phone, email or messaging about your enquiry;
  • To prepare and support your GST registration and related documentation;
  • To improve our website and services;
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Legal basis and consent

We process your information based on your consent (given when you submit an enquiry) and our legitimate interest in responding to and delivering the services you have requested. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with trusted service providers and partners who help us deliver our services (for example, documentation and compliance partners), and where required by law or a competent authority. These parties are expected to keep your information confidential.

5. Data retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal or regulatory requirements.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the performance of our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
